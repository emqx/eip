Operational boundaries
- Certificate reload validates the whole replacement; failure retains the working config.
- Existing connections keep their TLS state; new connections use the new config.
- Reload and restart invalidate caches/Tickets; no cross-node or OTP resumption sharing.
- Session cache is bounded; stateless Ticket rotation uses upstream ring Ticketer defaults.
- 0RTT stays disabled. Certificate, CRL and OCSP refresh is file-based and manual.
- TLS1.0/1.1, external PSK, legacy suites, encrypted keys, managed certificates and PROXY remain unsupported.
- No FIPS/PQ provider, full OTP option parity, hot native upgrade or Dashboard listener type.
- No TLS-Anvil/BoGo/x509-limbo, long-duration or million-connection qualification.
- No new performance measurements. Other shared-host workloads were left running.
- Original emqx_connection hashes and zero remaining channels/resources/adapters verified.
- No commits or pushes. Native upgrade requires a fresh BEAM VM.
